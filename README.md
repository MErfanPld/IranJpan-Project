# 🇮🇷 🇯🇵 Iran Japan Chamber of Commerce Website

A simple and professional business platform developed for the **Iran-Japan Chamber of Commerce** to support communication, business relations, and economic cooperation between Iranian and Japanese companies.

The website is designed to provide information about chamber activities, services, events, and international business opportunities.

---

# 🚀 Features

- 🌍 Multi-page Business Website
- 📰 News & Events Section
- 🏢 Chamber Introduction
- 📞 Contact & Communication Pages
- 📅 Event Management
- 🤝 Business Cooperation Information
- 📱 Responsive Design
- ⚡ Clean & Modern UI

---

# 🛠 Tech Stack

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- Django

---

# 🌐 Purpose

This platform was developed to help improve:

- Business Networking
- International Communication
- Trade Information Sharing
- Chamber Activities Management
- Economic Cooperation
